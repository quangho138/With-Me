Nature sounds (waves, birds, forest, rain, fire): Mixkit free sound effects, one per scene, under the Sound Effects Free License (commercial use allowed). Each is a stretch of at most 45 s with softened highs, kept quiet so it stays in the background (the fire's wind swoosh filtered and gated out between crackles), with a crossfaded loop seam, built by tool/make_nature_sounds.py and shipped as AAC .m4a (tool/compress_media.sh). Sources are in tool/media-source/README.md. Mixkit Free License: https://mixkit.co/license/ (no attribution required; do not redistribute the raw files on their own).

sigh-breath.wav: a generated breath sound, filling each phase of the Physiological Sigh. See SIGH-CREDITS.md.

voice/<language>/: the companion's spoken lines in Adrian's own voice, in English (en), Spanish (es), Hindi (hi), Arabic (ar), French (fr), Portuguese (pt), Russian (ru), Japanese (ja), Chinese (zh, also used for Traditional Chinese), German (de), Turkish (tr), Swahili (sw) and Korean (ko). Bengali, Indonesian, Urdu, Marathi, Vietnamese, Telugu, Filipino and Tamil have no recordings, because Chatterbox can't speak them, so the device's voice reads those lines. Adrian recorded a 9-second reference of his voice through free CapCut voice filters (2026-10-18), and Chatterbox Multilingual (Resemble AI, MIT licence, commercial use allowed) read each line in that voice, built by tool/make_voice_lines.py. Every file carries Resemble's inaudible PerTh watermark. Where the model added sounds after the line, the clip was trimmed; the other languages were made on 2026-10-18, and Chinese, German, Turkish, Swahili and Korean on 2026-10-19. checkin/page1 is the greeting; page2 to page10 follow the pages in order. page6_<area> follows the area picked on page 5 (home, work, school, social), and page8_<kind> follows the choice on page 7 (body, feelings, mind, behavior). Page 11 (Strategy details) has no recording. home/welcome_back.mp3 is the welcome on the home screen ("Welcome back!", without a name so it suits everyone).

The earlier English recordings, made by Adrian with fish.audio (2026-10-09 and 2026-10-12), are kept in tool/media-source/fish-audio-original/ and no longer ship with the app.
