# Delete your With Me account

With Me is a wellbeing companion app by HR Vision Consulting.

## In the app

1. Open With Me and sign in.
2. Open Settings.
3. Tap "Delete my account" and confirm.

This deletes your online account and everything stored with it (check-ins, journal, exercises, free tries, your name and email), and everything on that phone. It cannot be undone. If you want a copy first, use "Export my data" in Settings before deleting.

## Without the app

Email hrrhvisionconsulting@gmail.com from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We delete the account and all its data, and reply when it is done.

## What is kept

Nothing about your account is kept after deletion. If you bought With Me Plus, Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's rules; cancel the subscription in Google Play so it does not renew.
