# A calm daily check-in, with a friend beside you

With Me is a wellbeing companion app. A friendly companion greets you each day, asks how you are feeling, notices what is weighing on you, and suggests one small step that helps, such as a short breathing exercise.

## What you can do

<div class="card" markdown="1">

- **Check in each day**: your mood, your stress, where it comes from, and one small step for today.
- **Reflect** with one gentle question for each of who, what, when, where and why.
- **Calm down** with guided breathing, with nature sounds and video if you like.
- **See your days** on a calendar, and how your mood and stress change over a week, a month or a year.
- **Get a gentle reminder** at the time you choose, if you want one.
- **Use it in your language**: 22 languages, many with a recorded voice.

</div>

## Your data stays yours

<div class="card" markdown="1">

You can use With Me without an account, and then everything stays on your phone. With an account, your data follows you to a new phone. Only you can see it: never other people, and never your employer. No ads, no selling, no tracking. You can export or delete everything at any time. The [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) explains it all in plain words.

</div>

## Not a replacement for care

<div class="card" markdown="1">

With Me is a daily companion, not medical care, therapy or a crisis service. If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call your local emergency number. In the United States you can call or text 988. You can find a free crisis line in your country at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).

</div>
