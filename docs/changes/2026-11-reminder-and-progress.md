# Stay on Track in the new look, Your Progress removed (November 2026)

Two screens were still drawn in the old look.

- **Stay on Track** (opened from "Set a reminder" on Your day, and after a
  guided session) now uses the same parts as Your day and the Dashboard:
  the painted sunset, the chunky title, a cream panel of choice tiles with
  coloured markers and a tick, a switch row for Every day, the companion,
  and the teal pill. What each choice does is unchanged.
- **Your Progress** is gone. The round button in the Dashboard's top right
  corner is removed, and so are the screen and its `/progress` route, so
  nothing leads there. The weekly insight function stays: the weekly
  summary notice still uses it.
